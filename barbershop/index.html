The house

Built for the chair, not the clock.

We opened in 2012 with one chair and a neon sign in the window. Now there are four chairs, four barbers and a lot more neon. The rules haven't changed.

We book enough time for the cut you actually want, warm the towels before you sit down, and finish with the mirror held behind your head so you can see the back. If it isn't right yet, we keep going.
