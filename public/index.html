    Gate automation

    The barrier opens before the driver reaches for a ticket.

    Parkareto reads the plate, checks it against subscribers and reservations, issues a ticket and opens the barrier. When a plate can't be read, the operator types it at the manual gate. At the exit, a USB or Bluetooth scanner reads the ticket QR from any screen, with no field to click first.

    • Subscribers and reservations recognised at the gate

      Questions

      Before you ask.

      Which cameras and barriers does Parkareto work with?

      Parkareto connects to ANPR cameras and barrier controllers at your entry and exit lanes. Tell us what is installed today and we'll confirm compatibility before you sign up. We can also supply and install hardware.

      What happens if a plate can't be read?

      The gate operator types the plate at the manual gate and chooses Entry or Exit. The override is recorded in the Activity log with the user's name.

      How does the exit work without a cashier?

      The driver scans the ticket QR or barcode at the exit. A USB or Bluetooth scanner works from any screen in Parkareto, so nobody needs to click into a field first.

      Is myDATA transmission included?

      Yes, on every plan. Receipts are sent to AADE's myDATA and the ΜΑΡΚ is stored with the ticket. You need your own AADE credentials, which we help you set up.

      Can I design several floors?

      Yes. The Floor Designer supports multiple floors (Όροφος 1, 2, −1 …) and zones, with bay types for available, reserved and disabled spaces.

      Can I move my data or keep a backup?

      You can export a full backup at any time and restore it on another machine.

      Can I change plans later?

      Yes. Upgrade or downgrade from Settings; the new price applies from the next billing period.
