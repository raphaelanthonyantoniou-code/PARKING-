Legal bases

  • Providing your account and bookings: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Security of accounts, fraud and abuse prevention: our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Keeping booking and receipt records: legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Who we share it with

  • The car park you book, so they can let you in and keep their records.
  • Our email provider, to deliver sign-in codes and security alerts, and our hosting provider. They act on our instructions only.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We don't sell your data and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. It is HttpOnly, so scripts can't read it. No consent is needed for it because the site can't work securely without it.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to processing, and restrict it. You can edit your profile and vehicles, sign out devices and delete your account yourself from the Account & security page. For anything else write to [privacy@your-domain.gr]. You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (Αρχή Προστασίας Δεδομένων Προσωπικού Χαρακτήρα, www.dpa.gr).

Security

Every sign-in needs a second step: a code from an authenticator app or one we email you. Passwords are hashed with scrypt, authenticator keys are encrypted, connections use HTTPS, and repeated failed attempts are blocked.
