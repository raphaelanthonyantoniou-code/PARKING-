Street parking in central Athens

Not every trip ends in a garage. Here is what to know about parking on the street in the centre.

Controlled parking zones

Many central neighbourhoods use a controlled-parking system with resident and visitor spaces. Check street signs for hours and maximum stay, then start a session in the app.

Daktylios (traffic ring)

The central Athens ring restricts private cars on certain weekdays based on plate number. Park outside it at a park & ride and take the metro in.

Park & Ride

Cheap all-day parking near metro stations like Marousi and Neos Kosmos. Leave the car, ride Line 1, 2 or 3 to the centre.

FAQ

Can I cancel a reservation?

Yes — free cancellation up to 30 minutes before your start time, directly from “My bookings”.

What if I stay longer than booked?

Overtime is billed at the hourly rate, rounded to 15 minutes, and capped at the daily rate.

Do you support Greek plates and foreign plates?

Both. Enter the plate exactly as shown; Greek letters (e.g. ΙΚΧ) and Latin letters are both accepted.

Can my company book for its drivers?

Yes. Business accounts get monthly invoicing with VAT number (ΑΦΜ), multiple drivers and spending limits.

Is the airport covered?

Yes, long-term parking at Athens International Airport can be booked for multiple days with the daily rate applied.
